A Dirty Little Secret

I've spent most of my life addicted to stories. Stories about me. Stories about others. Stories about reality itself. Tragically, while lost inside of those fictions, my literal life was wasting away.

I was not alone.

In every moment of every day, a frightening number of people are addicted to stories — disconnected from the reality their bodies occupy while story-induced dissociation robs them of their life.

“I'm on a mission to help others awaken, move from disillusionment to clarity, make contact with what is most real, and live a life they can joyously call their own.”
